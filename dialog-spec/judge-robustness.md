**Scores on the degradation lens**

**Concept 1, "the-vehicle": 7/10.** It is the only one of the three that answers the two hardest clauses in the lens. *Model enquiry with no vehicle:* it routes `ModelEnquiryDialog`'s rail through `LINEUP[].image`, and those keys are real — `model-f-150`, `model-bronco`, 17 of them, present in `src/assets/images.gen.ts` and typed at `src/lib/dealerContent.ts:40`. *Missing image on a feed:* it is the only concept that specifies the raw `<img src={vehicle.image}>` fallback matching `src/components/site/VehicleCard.tsx:105-113`. That matters more than it looks (see C2/C3 below). Where it loses points: the rail is content-hungry by construction. `sellerNotes` and `msrp` are both optional on the `Vehicle` type, and the `price`/`finance` rail spec rows are specified as "Listed price, MSRP, Below MSRP, Odometer" — with no `msrp` that is a four-row table rendering two rows, and §9's mitigation list covers notes and images but never covers this. It degrades gracefully rather than breaking, but it degrades toward a thin 280px column, and a two-column grid is simply the largest structural surface area of the three. Its §9 is also the only falsifiable acceptance test anyone wrote: "on `availability` at 375x812 with a keyboard open, `DialogBody` must not scroll at all."

**Concept 2, "the-conversation": 6/10.** On paper it is the most feed-proof content model in the set: the identity strip reads `{year} {model} {trim}` and `$price · miles · condition`, and every one of those is a **required** field on `Vehicle`. No `msrp`, no badges, no `sellerNotes`, and incentives appear on `finance` only as one optional paragraph, so the expired-incentive clause is a non-event and 400 vehicles is a non-event. Then it throws that away on three concrete breaks:

1. **It renders no photograph on a real feed.** `imageNameFromSrc` (`src/components/site/ResponsiveImage.tsx:74`) matches a filename prefix against bundled `IMAGE_KEYS`, so a remote feed URL returns `undefined` every time. C2's rule is "render frame 2 with `bg-surface-2` and no `<img>`. Never a broken image." That is 400 empty grey boxes, in the concept whose stated first-three-seconds move is *"a photograph of the vehicle they were just looking at."*
2. **It never designs the no-vehicle case.** `ModelEnquiryDialog` appears once, in a bug footnote. There is no thumbnail source, no price line, and all six drafts interpolate `{y} {model} {trim}` — the entire angle has no defined form without a vehicle.
3. **It leaves double validation on phone.** `Field` defaults `required = true` and `Input` passes it to the DOM (`src/components/ledger/Field.tsx:103`). C2 adds no `noValidate`, so the browser's unstyled bubble fires before the error strip it just redesigned ever renders.

It is also the sharpest reader of the codebase: it is the only one that caught the em dash inside `smsLink` (`src/lib/leads.ts:82`, `Hi AM Ford — I'm interested in...`), a live violation of the copy rule, and the only one with a DEV-only strip naming the two missing env vars.

**Concept 3, "the-deal": 4/10.** This is the concept the lens is built to kill, and it knows, which earns it honesty points and no survival points. Its §9 does the arithmetic correctly: all four `INCENTIVES` carry `endsOn: "2026-08-31"`, today is 2026-08-14, so on 1 September `activeIncentives(new Date())` returns `[]` and the named angle disappears with no deploy. What §9 misses is worse: the ledger is also gated on `condition === "New"`, so on any real 400-vehicle feed the angle is absent for the used majority **today**, not in 17 days. Second, it promotes the least trustworthy field in the dataset to the second-largest element on screen — `VehicleCard.tsx:56-60` already passes `showSaving={false}` on the homepage because "the msrp values in the placeholder records are not real numbers," and a feed `msrp` is typically a base figure that yields an overstated green "$3,425 below MSRP" on every dialog. That is a claims problem, not a layout problem. Third, it shares C2's image flaw ("1e–1f do not render at all"), so no photograph on a feed, with a silent full-width reflow. Fourth, `ModelEnquiryDialog` gets "same header treatment, no vehicle figure block," which is precisely the lone-title header it was supposed to fix. Its floor state — a tinted band over a plain form — is where the client's complaint started.

**Strongest single idea in each**

- **C1:** the rail as a non-swapping DOM node. Column 1 is the same node through idle, sending, success and failure, so the success panel cannot shrink for structural reasons rather than because a measured `minHeight` was pinned to it. C3 solves the same defect by capturing `offsetHeight`; C1 solves it by making it impossible.
- **C2 (would not ship):** the payload inversion plus the `edited` flag. Today `src/components/lead/VehicleLeadDialog.tsx:155-157` sends `` `${cfg.cta} — ${title} ($${price}). ${message}` ``, so the sales desk reads a robot before it reads a human. Putting the customer's sentence first, metadata on its own line, and appending `· draft not edited` when the draft was sent untouched is the only idea in all three that makes the design's own stated risk measurable from the CRM.
- **C3 (would not ship):** the dateless floor. When there is no derived saving and no active programme, print one line built from `DELIVERY_SHORT` and `PRICING_STANCE`. Both are true in September, both are dealership-owned, neither needs a programme sheet. Paired with a DEV `console.warn` when `activeIncentives()` is empty, it makes an operational data dependency visible to the next developer instead of to the customer.

**Ship Concept 1.** It is the only one that is still recognisably itself when the vehicle is a feed row with no `msrp`, no notes and a remote image URL, and the only one that has a designed answer for the no-vehicle model enquiry. Graft eight things onto it:

1. **From C2, the required-fields-only content floor.** Rebuild the rail so it is complete from non-optional data alone: photo, `{year} Ford`, `{model} {trim}`, price, and four spec rows drawn from `miles`/`drivetrain`/`mpg`/`horsepower`. Replace C1's `price` and `finance` rail rows ("Listed price, MSRP, Below MSRP") with msrp-independent rows, and render the struck MSRP and the saving line as an optional pair above the table, gated exactly as `VehicleCard` gates them, carrying the homepage's `showSaving` escape hatch into the dialog.
2. **From C3, the dateless floor line and the DEV warn.** C1 correctly renders nothing when `activeIncentives()` is empty; on `price` and `finance`, put C3's one-line `DELIVERY_SHORT` + `PRICING_STANCE` statement in that slot so the position is never a hole, and add the `import.meta.env.DEV` warn.
3. **From C2, the DEV env-var strip** naming `VITE_SUPABASE_URL` and `VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`, layered on top of C1's already-correct render-time `leadStorageConfigured` branch.
4. **From C2, the payload inversion and the `smsLink` em dash fix** at `src/lib/leads.ts:82-83`. C1 does not touch the message shape at all, and the current shape is wrong.
5. **Reject C2's and C3's image fallback outright, and extend C1's.** Keep the raw `<img>` rung, then add a third: `onError` drops the photograph and the rail leads with the identity block. C1's spec stops at "no usable image" without saying how that is detected, and a feed URL can 404.
6. **From C3, the CSS-only bottom dock** (`inset-x-0 bottom-0 ... sm:left-1/2 sm:top-1/2 ...` on the same node), not C2's separate `size="docked"` branch. Same node means a resize across 640px never remounts the form and never discards typed values. Then take C2's one-line `interactive-widget=resizes-content` fix at `src/routes/__root.tsx:137`, which is the actual reason a pinned footer survives the Android keyboard.
7. **Keep C1's `<form noValidate>` and focus-to-first-invalid.** Both other concepts leave `Field`'s `required = true` default wired to the native control.
8. **Cut from C1: the `test_drive` date and time fields and the `QuickLeadInput` widening.** C2's reasoning wins — there is no calendar in this system and a picker implies availability the desk never agreed to. It is the one place C1 adds surface area this lens will punish, and the Sunday validation rule goes with it.
