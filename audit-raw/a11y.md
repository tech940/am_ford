## 2. Ranked findings

### F1 — OTPPopup is unlabelled and non-modal. **Blocker.** SC 1.3.1, 3.3.2, 4.1.2, 2.1.2, 2.4.3 (A)
`src/components/popups/OTPPopup.tsx:283` — the overlay/card are bare `<div>`s: no `role="dialog"`, no `aria-modal`, no accessible name.
All six fields have a `<label>` that neither wraps the input nor carries `htmlFor`, so **none of them has an accessible name**:
`:334-345` first name · `:363-374` last name · `:392-403` preferred contact `<select>` · `:423-434` phone · `:457-468` email · `:486-497` comments.
No focus move-in, no trap, no restore, no Escape, no scroll lock anywhere in the file. This is the gate on "Get Price" from both the inventory grid (`inventory.tsx:2270`) and the VDP (`vehicle.$id.tsx:1009`), so it is the highest-traffic broken surface on the site.

### F2 — LeadCaptureModal has no dialog semantics and an unnamed close button. **Blocker.** SC 4.1.2, 1.3.1, 2.1.2, 2.4.3 (A)
`src/components/site/LeadCaptureModal.tsx:123-128` — the modal window is a plain `motion.div`: no `role`, no `aria-modal`, no label.
`:140-145` close button contains only a lucide `<X/>` (lucide-react ships **no** `aria-hidden`, and the svg has no text) → **accessible name is empty**.
Orphan labels at `:246`, `:263`, `:281`, `:301`, `:344` — only the Preferred Time `<select>` (`:316-336`) is wired correctly, which shows the fix pattern already exists in-file.
No Escape handler, no scroll lock, no focus management (the only effect is the mode sync at `:43-45`). `:237-241` error has no `role="alert"`. Reached from 4 entry points (test drive, quote, special order, financing pre-approval).

### F3 — ExitIntentOffer: unsolicited modal that never receives focus. **Blocker.** SC 2.4.3, 4.1.2 (A), 2.4.11 (AA)
`src/components/convert/ExitIntentOffer.tsx:143-151` declares `aria-modal="true"` but nothing moves focus into the dialog and there is no trap. `aria-modal` tells AT to hide the rest of the page; because focus never enters, a screen-reader user gets **no notification that anything opened** and continues tabbing through background content the AT now reports as hidden. Worst case of the "aria-modal without trap" pattern because this dialog appears unrequested (mouse-leave `:73-75`, or 70 % scroll depth on mobile `:77-81`). Escape (`:98-101`) and scroll lock (`:96`) are correct; labels and `role="alert"` (`:210`) are correct.

### F4 — Inventory hero "Claim Offer" form: 4 controls with no accessible name. **Blocker.** SC 1.3.1, 3.3.2, 4.1.2 (A)
`src/routes/inventory.tsx:1168` / `:1184` / `:1201` / `:1218` — orphan `<label>`s; the inputs at `:1173` and `:1189` and the selects at `:1204` and `:1221` carry only a placeholder. This is the first form in the page's tab order and it submits into OTPPopup (`:1157-1163`). *(Side note, non-a11y: those two inputs are uncontrolled and their values are never read.)*

### F5 — TradeValuatorModal: 4 placeholder-only inputs; wizard steps silently drop focus. **High.** SC 3.3.2, 4.1.2 (A), 4.1.3 (AA)
`src/components/convert/TradeValuatorModal.tsx:383-391` (model), `:404-413` (miles), `:492-499` (name), `:506-515` (phone) — no `<label>`, no `aria-label`; `<h4>` above is not programmatically associated.
`:347-357`, `:365-377`, `:444-469` — selecting a year/make/condition remounts the step (`key` on `:294`), unmounting the clicked button, so focus falls to `<body>`. Nothing announces the new step; `:264-267` "Step N of 6" is not a live region and the progress bar `:279-286` has no `role="progressbar"`.
Errors at `:328-334` and `:480-484` have no `role="alert"`; the revealed estimate `:300-341` is not announced.
Everything else here is exemplary — focus in/restore `:120-126`, a trap that correctly handles focus landing *outside* the root `:151-162`, Escape `:131`, scroll lock `:110-116`. **Use this file's trap, not OfferPopup's, as the trap reference.**

### F6 — QuickEnquiryModal: correct semantics, zero focus management. **High.** SC 2.4.3, 2.1.2 (A), 4.1.3 (AA)
`src/components/convert/QuickEnquiryModal.tsx:139-147` has the full `role`/`aria-modal`/`aria-labelledby` triple, and Escape + scroll lock at `:89-100`, but no focus move-in, no trap, and no restore — so Tab walks straight out into the page behind while `aria-modal` claims it is hidden. `:222-226` error is not announced (`role="alert"` missing — compare `OfferPopup.tsx:334`). Success panel `:169-196` is not announced and does not receive focus.

### F7 — Inventory filter drawer: not a dialog, 9 orphan labels, state-only-by-colour pills. **High.** SC 1.3.1, 4.1.2, 2.1.2 (A)
`src/routes/inventory.tsx:1918-1924` — `<motion.aside>` with no `role="dialog"`, no `aria-modal`, no accessible name; no focus move-in/trap/restore (Escape `:932-938` and scroll lock `:941-950` are present).
`:1949`, `:1977`, `:2003`, `:2033`, `:2056`, `:2082`, `:2108`, `:2133`, `:2158` — `<label>` used as a group heading with no associated control. Should be `<fieldset>`/`<legend>` or `role="group"` + `aria-labelledby`.
Every filter pill (`:1954`, `:1982`, `:2008`, `:2061`, `:2113`, `:2165`, and the toolbar chips `:1337`) conveys selection **only** through background colour — no `aria-pressed`. Toolbar search `:1318` has no label (placeholder only) and its clear button `:1325` has no `aria-label`.
Result-count changes (`:1467-1484`) and the empty state (`:1510`) are in no live region, so filtering is silent — SC 4.1.3.

### F8 — ChatWidget declares `aria-modal="true"` on a non-modal panel. **High.** SC 4.1.2 (A), 4.1.3 (AA)
`src/components/convert/ChatWidget.tsx:387-390` — the panel is a corner sheet: the page behind stays scrollable and interactive (correctly, there is no scroll lock), yet `aria-modal="true"` instructs AT to hide everything else. Remove `aria-modal`, or make it genuinely modal. Focus never enters the panel on open (`:369-376`) and is never restored to the launcher on close.
`:417` the conversation container is not `role="log"` / `aria-live="polite"`, so scripted replies appended after the 650 ms typing delay (`:264-273`) are never announced. `:179` error is a plain `<p>`. Launcher `:365-381` lacks `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`.

### F9 — OfferPopup residual gaps (reference implementation). **Medium.** SC 2.4.3 (A), 4.1.3 (AA)
Verified correct: dialog triple `:119-124`, focus in + restore `:58-64`, scroll lock `:67-73`, Escape with capture-phase `stopPropagation` `:76-81`, backdrop dismiss on `mousedown` target check `:105-107`, `htmlFor`/`id` on all four fields `:277-330`, `role="alert"` `:334`, `aria-hidden` on all decorative glyphs. Three gaps remain:
1. **Focus is lost on submit.** `:203` and `:244` both return `<ModalShell>` at the same position, so React reconciles rather than remounts — the `[]`-deps effect at `:58-64` never re-runs. The focused submit button unmounts and focus falls to `<body>`, outside the dialog.
2. **The trap only fires at the edges.** `:88-93` acts only when `document.activeElement` is exactly `first` or `last`; once focus is on `<body>` (see 1), Tab escapes into the page behind. `TradeValuatorModal.tsx:152-162` shows the `outside` check that fixes this.
3. **The confirmation is not announced.** `:223-228` "Request received" has no `role="status"` and receives no focus.

### F10 — Field errors signalled by transient colour only. **Medium.** SC 3.3.1, 1.4.1 (A)
`OTPPopup.tsx:218-224` and `TradeOfferPopup.tsx:106-110` — a failed submit pushes field names into `invalidFields`, paints a red border (`OTPPopup.tsx:269`, `TradeOfferPopup.tsx:77-80`), and **clears it after 410 ms**. No text message is produced for missing first name or short phone, no `aria-invalid`, no `aria-describedby`, no `required`/`aria-required`. A non-sighted user submits and receives nothing at all; a sighted user has 410 ms to notice.

### F11 — InspectionUnlock: labels wrap an icon, not text. **Medium.** SC 1.3.1, 3.3.2, 4.1.2 (A), 4.1.3 (AA)
`src/routes/vehicle.$id.tsx:1465-1475` and `:1476-1485` — `<label className="relative block">` wraps the input (implicit association is fine) but its only other child is a lucide icon, so the computed name is empty. `:1499` error is a plain `<p>`. The locked→unlocked swap `:1416` is not announced. The trigger `:1500` is a bare `<button>` outside any `<form>`, so Enter in the email field does nothing.

### F12 — PaymentCalculator range inputs have no accessible name. **Medium.** SC 1.3.1, 4.1.2 (A)
`src/routes/vehicle.$id.tsx:1683-1691` — `<input type="range">` with no `id`/`htmlFor`/`aria-label`; the label text lives in a sibling `<p>` at `:1678`. Affects both instances (`:1611` down payment, `:1642` APR). Value readout `:1681` is not linked via `aria-describedby`. Term chips `:1625-1638` have no `aria-pressed` and no group label.

### F13 — SiteNav mobile sheet is not a dialog; triggers have no expanded state. **Medium.** SC 4.1.2 (A), 2.1.2 (A)
`src/components/site/SiteNav.tsx:247-251` — the sheet panel has no `role="dialog"`, no `aria-modal`, no accessible name; no Escape, no scroll lock, no focus move-in/restore. The `inert={!open}` at `:233` correctly keeps it out of the tab order when shut (React 19 supports the boolean prop), and the backdrop click works, so this is a semantics/announcement failure rather than a keyboard trap.
`:221-227` hamburger has `aria-label="Open menu"` but no `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`. `:157-167` the three desktop dropdown triggers have no `aria-expanded`/`aria-haspopup` — the CSS `group-focus-within` reveal is genuinely keyboard-operable and well documented at `:17-33`, but a screen-reader user gets no warning that ~25 links follow the trigger. The mobile `<details>`/`<summary>` at `:282-286` is correct and needs no change.

### F14 — CompareModal: correct semantics, no focus management. **Low.** SC 2.4.3, 2.1.2 (A)
`src/routes/inventory.tsx:2379-2385` has `role`/`aria-modal`/`aria-label`, Escape and scroll lock at `:2360-2368`, but no focus move-in, trap, or restore.

### F15 — Inventory FAQ: collapsed answers stay exposed to AT. **Low.** SC 1.3.1, 4.1.2 (A)
`src/routes/inventory.tsx:1877-1889` — the panel collapses via `grid-rows-[0fr]` + `overflow-hidden`, which yields zero height but leaves the text in the accessibility tree while the trigger reports `aria-expanded={false}` (`:1852`). The SEO constraint documented at `:1867-1876` (answers must be in the served HTML) is real, but `hidden="until-found"` or `visibility: hidden` satisfies both. The `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls` wiring itself is correct. The VDP equivalent (`vehicle.$id.tsx:914-933`, native `<details>`) has no such problem and is the better pattern.

### F16 — Focus indicator is a border-colour change only. **Low.** SC 2.4.7 (AA), 1.4.11 (AA)
`LeadCaptureModal.tsx:257,274,292,310,329,360`, `vehicle.$id.tsx:1473,1483`, and `styles.css:538-542` all pair `outline: none` with a `focus:border-primary` swap (`#e2e8f0` → `#05214f`) and no ring. A 1.5 px border recolour is a weak indicator against the adjacent background. Every other surface in scope pairs `outline-none` with a `focus:ring-*` and is fine.

### F17 — No surface announces its success state. **Low.** SC 4.1.3 (AA)
Confirmation panels swap in silently with no `role="status"` and no focus move: `OfferPopup.tsx:203`, `OTPPopup.tsx:601`, `TradeOfferPopup.tsx:135`, `QuickEnquiryModal.tsx:169`, `ExitIntentOffer.tsx:182`, `TradeValuatorModal.tsx:300`, `LeadCaptureModal.tsx:150`, `ChatWidget.tsx:104`, `vehicle.$id.tsx:1416`. One shared fix.

## 3. Suggested remediation order

1. **Extract `ModalShell` from `OfferPopup.tsx:43-140` into a shared component**, first patching the three F9 gaps (re-run focus on children change or key the shell per step; adopt `TradeValuatorModal.tsx:151-162`'s `outside` check; add `role="status"` to confirmations). Then wrap OTPPopup, TradeOfferPopup, LeadCaptureModal, the inventory filter drawer, and CompareModal in it — that single change closes F1, F2, F7, F14 and the dialog half of F5/F6.
2. **Add focus move-in + trap + restore** to QuickEnquiryModal and ExitIntentOffer (F3, F6); drop `aria-modal` from ChatWidget (F8).
3. **Label sweep** — `useId()` + `htmlFor`/`id` on the 20 unnamed controls in F1, F4, F5, F11, F12.
4. **Error/status sweep** — `role="alert"` on the 6 error nodes; replace the 410 ms colour flash with persistent messages + `aria-invalid`/`aria-describedby` (F10, F17).
5. `aria-pressed` on filter/term pills, `aria-expanded` on nav and drawer triggers, live region on the inventory result count (F7, F12, F13).
